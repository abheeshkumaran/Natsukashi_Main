Every beautiful journey begins with a dream. For as long as I can remember, sarees have held a special place in my heart. To me, they are more than garments—they are timeless expressions of culture, artistry, and the memories woven into every thread. My love for India’s traditional weaves inspired a dream that stayed with me for years: to create a brand that celebrates the richness of our textile heritage.

In 2026, that dream became a reality with the birth of Natsukashii. Founded by a girl with an unwavering passion for sarees, Natsukashii was created to bring together thoughtfully curated collections from the traditional weaving traditions of India. From the timeless elegance of Kerala Kasavu to the artistry of handwoven treasures from across the country, every piece is carefully selected for its authenticity, craftsmanship, and enduring beauty.

Like every dream, mine needed someone who believed in it before the world did. My husband became that unwavering support. He encouraged me to take the first step, believed in my vision even when it was just an idea, and gave me the confidence to turn a long-held dream into reality. Natsukashii is as much a journey of love, faith, and encouragement as it is a celebration of Indian textiles.

The name Natsukashii comes from the Japanese word that evokes a warm sense of nostalgia—a beautiful memory that brings a smile. That feeling lies at the heart of our brand. We believe every saree becomes part of life’s most cherished moments—festivals, weddings, celebrations, and memories that last a lifetime.

At Natsukashii, we honour the artisans and weavers whose craftsmanship keeps India’s rich textile traditions alive. Every collection is curated with care, celebrating authenticity, heritage, and timeless elegance.

What began as a personal dream has become a heartfelt journey to share India’s finest traditional weaves with women who value beauty, culture, and craftsmanship.
